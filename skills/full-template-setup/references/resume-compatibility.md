# Resume compatibility

Load this reference only after the existing resume and the current site/profile
surfaces have been inspected.

## Inspect before presenting

Read the supplied resume completely. Inspect the current factual-profile fields,
site sections, navigation, contact presentation, assets, and assistant grounding.
Compare meaning, not merely headings or wording.

Classify each material item as:

- fits an existing current-site surface;
- needed by the current site but missing or unclear;
- not represented by the current site; or
- privacy-sensitive or unsuitable for public presentation without a decision.

Do not silently fix contradictions, rewrite claims, combine unrelated sections,
or assume unsupported content is unimportant.

## Present the compatibility summary

Use these adopter-facing headings:

1. Content that fits the current site.
2. Information the site needs but the resume does not clearly provide.
3. Resume content the current site does not currently represent.
4. Privacy or public-presentation concerns.

Under each heading, cite the resume content in concise plain language and state
whether confirmation or a decision is needed. Avoid leading with schema,
field-model, or implementation terminology. Describe content as fitting the
current site, missing for the site, or requiring a future site extension.

After the first heading, ask: “I found the following content that fits the
current site. Is this summary factually correct?”

Do not continue to presentation choices until material factual corrections are
resolved or explicitly recorded as blockers.

## Decision rules

- Map clearly equivalent confirmed resume data to existing profile fields.
- Propose a concise representation only when it preserves meaning, then request
  confirmation.
- Recommend initial omission when content duplicates stronger material, has low
  value for the stated goal, creates privacy risk, or requires disproportionate
  complexity.
- Recommend a future site extension when unsupported content is important to the
  adopter's profession, audience, credibility, or primary site goal.
- Never interpret unsupported content as authorization to expand the profile or
  site.
- Do not automatically combine unrelated resume sections or ask the adopter to
  combine them merely to fit the default template.
- Evaluate education, certifications, awards, volunteering, services, and other
  unsupported content against the adopter's profession, audience, credibility,
  and primary goal rather than assuming it should be omitted.
- Record omissions, rationale, future-extension notes, and unresolved claims
  separately in the interview ledger and final setup brief.

## Recommendation format

For every unsupported item, record:

- the content and why it matters to the adopter's stated goal;
- the labeled recommendation: omit initially, future site extension, or a concise
  fit in an existing surface;
- the practical consequence of that recommendation;
- the adopter's decision; and
- any exact wording still requiring confirmation.

Use the exact compatibility and unsupported-content questions from
[Question bank](question-bank.md). Complete this stage only after the adopter
confirms the material summary and every unsupported-content and privacy decision
is recorded.
